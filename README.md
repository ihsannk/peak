# About PEAK

## Pharmacy Enforcement Archive of Knowledge

**PEAK** (Pharmacy Enforcement Archive of Knowledge) is a digital reference platform developed to support officers and personnel involved in **pharmacy enforcement and related legal work**.

The platform brings together relevant legislation, regulations, orders, and other legal reference materials in one organised and searchable location, making it easier to locate information when it is needed.

### Our Purpose

Pharmacy enforcement work often requires quick access to legislation and specific provisions. PEAK is designed to simplify that process by providing a centralised reference point for commonly used legal documents.

The platform aims to:

* Provide convenient access to relevant pharmacy enforcement legislation.
* Make legal documents easier to search and navigate.
* Reduce the time required to locate specific provisions.
* Support officers in their day-to-day enforcement and legal work.
* Preserve useful legal reference materials in an organised digital archive.
* Encourage consistent and informed reference to the applicable legislation.

### What You Can Find

PEAK currently organises reference materials into three main categories:

**Acts**
Key Acts relevant to pharmacy enforcement, criminal procedure, evidence, medicines, drugs, trade and other related matters.

**Regulations**
Regulations made under relevant Acts, including regulations relating to poisons, medicines, pharmacists, dangerous drugs and other enforcement matters.

**Orders**
Selected Orders and subsidiary legislation relevant to pharmacy enforcement activities.

### Search & Reference

PEAK is designed around a simple principle:

> **Find the information you need, when you need it.**

Users can search across selected legal documents and go directly to the relevant page containing the search term. The platform also provides document navigation and in-PDF search functions to support more detailed reading and reference.

### Who Is PEAK For?

PEAK is primarily intended as a practical reference tool for personnel involved in:

* Pharmacy enforcement
* Legal and prosecution work
* Investigation
* Regulatory activities
* Compliance and inspection
* Policy and technical reference
* Training and professional development

### A Living Reference

PEAK is intended to develop alongside the needs of pharmacy enforcement personnel. Its contents and functionality may be updated from time to time to improve accessibility, usability and the availability of relevant reference materials.

Users should always refer to the **latest officially published legislation and authoritative sources** when making legal decisions or taking enforcement action.

### Disclaimer

PEAK is provided as a **reference and knowledge-support tool**. The materials available through this platform should not be regarded as a substitute for the official published versions of legislation or legal advice.

In the event of any discrepancy between a document available on PEAK and the official legislation, the **official published legislation shall prevail**.
